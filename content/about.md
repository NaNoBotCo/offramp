## Why Offrampt exists

It started with a payment. Paying someone based in Thailand who had no Thai bank turned out
to be cheaper in crypto than through US banking — and then the network confusion (native
Bitcoin versus a Base `0x` address) nearly stranded the money. That small, real headache
made the gap obvious: getting *paid* in crypto is easy, but turning it into money you can
spend **without a friendly local bank** is full of expensive traps nobody maps clearly.

So we mapped it. Offrampt is that map — starting where we live, in Thailand, and expanding to
other places nomads and expats land.

## Who it's for

- People who earn across borders and get paid in crypto or stablecoins.
- Expats, digital nomads, and travelers who **don't have a local bank account** — or don't
  want to route everything through one.
- Anyone who's stared at a wallet full of USDT and thought "…okay, but how do I buy
  groceries with this?"

## The bright line: we never touch your money

This matters, so it's stated plainly. Offrampt **informs and refers.** It does not hold,
move, exchange, convert, or take custody of anyone's crypto or cash — ever. You deal with
every exchange, P2P desk, and card issuer **directly**; we just tell you which roads exist
and where the potholes are.

That line is deliberate. The moment a site starts custodying or moving people's money, it
becomes a money transmitter and needs licensing. Offrampt stays on the *information* side of
that line — like a good travel guide tells you which trains run without selling you the
ticket.

## How Offrampt pays for itself

The tools you need to cash out — exchanges, P2P platforms, crypto debit cards, hardware
wallets — are some of the best-paying referral programs in crypto. When a link marked
%%MARK%% leads you to sign up, we may earn a commission at **no extra
cost to you.** It never changes what we recommend: we only put our name next to tools we'd
point a friend toward. Full details on the [[disclosure|disclosure page]].

We'd rather be paid by the products that genuinely solve your problem than wall the guides
behind a paywall. The guides stay free.

## How we write

- **Every guide is free**, and the safety facts come before the sales.
- **We mark our confidence.** Where something is our read rather than a hard fact, we tag it
  *(inference — confirm live)*. Crypto rules and product availability move fast; we'd rather
  point you to check than pretend to certainty.
- **This is not financial, tax, or legal advice.** We're travelers and builders, not
  licensed advisors. For anything that moves real money or touches your taxes, confirm with a
  professional.

and expat-logistics concierge in Chiang Mai. Same audience — location-independent people

you get paid and spend.
