## Affiliate / referral disclosure

Offrampt participates in affiliate and referral programs. Some links on this site — the ones
marked with %%MARK%% — are **affiliate links**. If you click one and
sign up for or buy a product, Offrampt may earn a commission or referral bonus, **at no extra
cost to you.**

This is how the site pays for itself and stays free to read.

**Our commitment:**

- We only link products we'd genuinely suggest to a friend in the same situation.
- A commission never changes our recommendation, our ranking, or what we tell you about a
  product's downsides.
- Where a link is a referral link, it's marked. Where we mention a tool without a referral
  relationship, we don't pretend otherwise.
- We disclose this on every page that carries affiliate links, in line with the US Federal
  Trade Commission's guidance on endorsements.

Some travel-adjacent links use the Travelpayouts network. Card issuers, exchanges, and other
services each run their own referral programs; where we hold a referral relationship, the
link is marked as above.

## Not financial, tax, or legal advice

Everything on Offrampt is **general information**, published for education. It is **not**:

- financial or investment advice,
- tax advice or preparation, or
- legal advice.

We are not licensed financial advisors, accountants, or lawyers, and nothing here is
tailored to your personal situation. Crypto is volatile and risky; rules, fees, and product
availability change constantly and vary by country. **Before you move money, confirm current
details with each provider and with a licensed professional** in your jurisdiction.

## We never touch your funds

Offrampt does not hold, move, exchange, convert, or take custody of your crypto or cash. We
publish information and refer you to third-party services you deal with **directly.** We are
not a bank, broker, exchange, money transmitter, or custodian, and we don't act as one.
