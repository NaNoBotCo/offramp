## Why this exists

Offrampt was born from a real moment: paying someone based in Thailand who has no Thai
bank turned out to be *cheaper in crypto* than through US banking — and then the network
confusion (native Bitcoin versus a Base `0x` address) nearly stranded the funds. That
lived problem is the whole point of this site. If you get paid across borders and don't
have a friendly local bank, you are the person we're writing for.

:::key The one rule that saves the most money
Crypto sent over the **wrong network** or to the wrong address is usually gone for
good — no bank to call, no reversal. Before every transfer, match the **coin, the
network, and the address format** on both ends. We repeat this everywhere because it's
the mistake that costs the most. See [[scams|Stay safe]].
:::
