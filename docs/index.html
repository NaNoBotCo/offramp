Why this exists

Offrampt was born from a real moment: paying someone based in Thailand who has no Thai bank turned out to be cheaper in crypto than through US banking — and then the network confusion (native Bitcoin versus a Base 0x address) nearly stranded the funds. That lived problem is the whole point of this site. If you get paid across borders and don't have a friendly local bank, you are the person we're writing for.

The one rule that saves the most money

Crypto sent over the wrong network or to the wrong address is usually gone for good — no bank to call, no reversal. Before every transfer, match the coin, the network, and the address format on both ends. We repeat this everywhere because it's the mistake that costs the most. See Stay safe.

How Offrampt makes money (and how it doesn't)

The tools you need to cash out without a bank — exchanges, P2P desks, crypto debit cards, hardware wallets — happen to be the crypto industry's best-paying referrals. So we can keep every guide free and still keep the lights on: when a link marked ↗ leads you to sign up, we may earn a commission, at no extra cost to you.

Offrampt never touches your money. We don't hold it, move it, exchange it, or take custody of anything. You deal with each exchange, desk, or card issuer directly — we just show you the road. That bright line is what keeps this a guide and not a licensed money business. Full affiliate disclosure.
