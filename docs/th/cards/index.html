TouristDigiPay (Thai SEC regulatory sandbox)

รายงานข่าว

A sandbox run under the Thai SEC with the Ministry of Finance, the Anti-Money Laundering Office and the Ministry of Tourism and Sports, announced 18 August 2025. A foreign visitor opens two accounts once in Thailand: one with an SEC-licensed digital asset exchange, broker or dealer, and one e-money wallet with a Bank of Thailand-licensed e-money operator. Crypto is converted to THB in the wallet and spent at merchants by QR; crypto is not paid to merchants and cash withdrawal is not permitted. Reported timeline: rules effective 24 September 2025; operator applications 25 September to 26 December 2025; go-live expected Q4 2025; sandbox period up to 18 months. Unused THB can be converted back to digital assets through the same operator and sent to the original wallet (Thairath, 18 August 2025). The scheme extends the earlier Phuket Sandbox (Thairath). Participants: Bitkub Exchange announced it joined, planning account opening for foreign visitors and e-money partners at airports (Bangkok Biz News press release, 20 August 2025); a January 2026 guide names SCBX's Rubie Wallet; no official participant list was read. Status as of September 2026: reported as a live pilot running into early 2027; the SEC's site returned HTTP 403 to the fetch, so current status and participant list are not confirmed.

ค่าใช้จ่าย: Limits reported: 50,000 THB per person per month for payments to small vendors (no know-your-merchant check); up to 500,000 THB per person per month at merchants that completed know-your-merchant. สำหรับชาวต่างชาติ: For foreign visitors staying in Thailand temporarily (AIM Bangkok's wording, paraphrased).
