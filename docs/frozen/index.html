Worse than today

Eight steps, each worse than the one before. Each has happened somewhere before. Nobody knows which step this one stops at.

  1. 0

    Paused

    What happens Today. Binance and Binance TH stop crypto withdrawals. You see your coins in the app. You cannot move them.

    Before Zipmex, an exchange with a large Thai business, stopped withdrawals on 20 July 2022. Thai withdrawals came back late the same day, except from one savings product.[483]

    What helps

    • An exchange posts its own state in its app and on its website.
    • A screenshot with the date showing is proof of a balance on that day.
    • The counter below adds up every place your money is.
  2. 1

    Weeks, not days

    What happens The pause goes on. Prices move while your coins cannot. If you sell a little each month to pay rent, the rent comes before the exchange opens.

    Before Celsius, a crypto lender, stopped withdrawals on 12 June 2022 and filed for bankruptcy on 13 July 2022.[491][492]

    What helps

    • The counter below shows how many months the money outside a frozen place pays for.
    • Two ways to baht through two different companies: one can freeze while the other runs.
  3. 2

    The promise falls short

    What happens Binance says it will cover every loss. About $6.3 billion is gone, about $3 billion is frozen, and its user fund (SAFU) held about $1 billion when last reported, in 2024. If the money falls short, customers get back a part, late, or a token that stands for the rest.

    Before Mt. Gox, once the largest bitcoin exchange, failed in February 2014. Repayments began on 5 July 2024, ten years later. The deadline has since moved to 31 October 2026, with many customers still waiting.[493][494][495][496][497][488]

    What helps

    • A claim runs on proof: statements, screenshots, deposit receipts, transaction IDs (TxID: the receipt number of a crypto transfer), and the exchange's emails.
    • Notices and payouts go to the email and phone number on the account.
  4. 3

    The run spreads

    What happens Fear moves to other exchanges. Many people withdraw at once; some exchanges slow or stop withdrawals, Thai ones too. Prices fall. A loan backed by crypto is sold off when the price falls.

    Before FTX: about $6 billion was withdrawn in 72 hours, and it filed for bankruptcy on 11 November 2022.[498][499][500]

    What helps

    • Each exchange sets a daily withdrawal limit, shown in its app.
    • A loan backed by crypto has a liquidation price: the price at which the lender sells the coins to pay itself back.
  5. 4

    USDT breaks

    What happens USDT is a coin meant to stay at one US dollar; it is 47% of Bitkub's own baht trading. Today Tether froze about $1.57 billion of it: one company can stop any USDT address. If people stop believing a dollar stands behind each coin, USDT sells for less than a dollar.

    Before TerraUSD, another dollar coin, lost its dollar value in May 2022; about $45 billion of market value went in a week.[501][502][487]

    What helps

    • The part of savings held in one coin is the part one company can stop.
    • The Offrampt home page shows the USDT-to-baht price, live.
  6. 5

    The bank door shuts

    What happens Selling to a stranger (P2P, person to person) can bring you baht from someone else's loss. The bank freezes your account. Since July 2026 the Bank of Thailand screens large USDT trades.

    Before By 30 June 2025 the SEC had blocked more than 29,000 crypto mule accounts (accounts used to move money taken by fraud) and frozen about ฿186 million.[503][77][81][82][105]

    What helps

    • A sale on an exchange licensed by the Thai SEC pays into a bank account in the seller's own name.
    • Sale records show where the baht came from.
  7. 6

    The fraud wave

    What happens Fake Binance support on LINE, Facebook and the phone. “Compensation” forms that ask for your login. “Recovery” services that want a fee first.

    Before On 29 October Binance told users not to trust “unofficial links, messages, or requests for account information”. The FBI says a person who offers to get lost crypto back may be running a second fraud.[486][442][429]

    What helps

    • Recovery words are the 12 or 24 words that open a wallet. Whoever has them can empty it.
    • An address typed by hand reaches the exchange; a link in a message can reach a copy of it.
    • Hotline 1441 answers 24 hours a day; police reports go in at thaipoliceonline.go.th.
  8. 7

    Your own phone

    What happens Binance's theft may have started on one laptop. Your phone and computer can open the same way: a stolen login, recovery words in a photo on iCloud or Google Photos, one password on two sites. Nobody can pause your own wallet.

    Before In November 2025 Thailand's cyber police (CCIB) worked a case where malware searched phones for Google Authenticator backups and recovery words, and attackers took over exchange accounts.[450][451][487]

    What helps

    • Recovery words on paper or metal stay off the internet. A photo of them goes up to iCloud or Google Photos.
    • SMS codes follow the phone number, and a SIM swap (moving a number to another SIM) hands them to someone else. An authenticator app stays on the phone.
    • Wallet-stealing apps arrive through links in chats and ads; a wallet maker's own page links its own app.
  9. 8

    The family

    What happens You go into hospital, or you die. Your family has no list, no keys and no idea who to call, and fraudsters find them first.

    What helps

    • A written list of where the coins are, and whom to call, lets a family act without the recovery words.
    • Hongdam's family plan does this with you, without your recovery words in plain sight.

Money gone?

Hotline 1441 answers 24 hours a day. Police reports go in at thaipoliceonline.go.th. Under the 2023 technology crime decree, a bank can freeze the account the money went to as soon as the victim asks, before a police report.[80]

Thai Police Online (online police report)

File a police report for online fraud and technology crime. Police name this site and hotline 1441 as the channels for online reports.

https://thaipoliceonline.go.th/

Hotline 1441

24-hour hotline for technology crime advice; the Ministry of Digital Economy and Society's AOC 1441 service coordinates freezing of scammers' bank accounts.

https://www.ccib.go.th/

Your bank's hotline (account freeze)

Under the 2023 technology crime decree, a victim can ask the bank to suspend the receiving account at once, without first filing a police report.

https://thaipublica.org/2023/03/electronic-crime-law-effective-now/

SEC complaint and tip-off centre 1207

Report a suspected unlicensed platform or investment solicitation by phone on 1207, by email to complaint@sec.or.th, or via SEC Live Chat.

https://market.sec.or.th/public/idisc/th/InvestorAlert
