The same coin travels on many networks. USDT alone sits on 51 of them; 52% is on Tron. The network has to match at both ends, or the coins arrive somewhere the receiver cannot reach.[422]

THE MISTAKE. A sender picks a network from a menu. The receiver's address has to be on that network. A 0x address is valid on Ethereum, BNB Smart Chain, Base, Arbitrum and a dozen more, so the address alone never says which one. Coins sent on a network the receiving exchange does not support can sit in an address nobody there watches.

Fees

A typical bitcoin payment is about 141 virtual bytes. The median fee rate over the last year was 1.0 sat/vB (a sat is one hundred-millionth of a bitcoin), so a payment cost roughly ฿3.55 at today's price. Exchanges charge their own withdrawal fee on top, often more than the network's.[423][92]
